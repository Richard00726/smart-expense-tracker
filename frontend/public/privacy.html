Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Cash & UPI Tracker. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and financial data. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Cash & UPI Tracker does not sell, lease, or share your financial data or email contents with any third parties or advertisers. Your bank statements and transaction details remain strictly confidential.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide automatic bank transaction tracking and expense management:

  • Account Details: Email address and username when you create an account.
  • Financial Transactions: Amount, transaction date, note/narration, reference number, and account balance derived from your official bank statements or manual entries.
  • Google Account Data (Optional): If you choose to enable automated Gmail Bank Auto-Sync, we access your connected Google account strictly through the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly scope.

2. Google User Data & Limited Use Disclosure

Our app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We strictly read only automated bank statement emails and PDF attachments from your bank (specifically Tamilnad Mercantile Bank - TMB).
  • We do NOT read your personal emails, chat messages, contacts, or any unrelated correspondence.
  • We do NOT use Google data to serve advertisements or train generalized AI models.
  • Tokens and credentials are securely encrypted and stored solely to enable background statement fetching.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To decrypt and parse your official bank statements using your personal statement password.
  • To automatically categorize your UPI and Cash expenditures.
  • To compute daily, monthly, and yearly expense summaries and budgets.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • You can disconnect your Google Account at any time with a single click.
  • You can completely delete your bank sync configuration and all imported transactions directly from the settings panel.
